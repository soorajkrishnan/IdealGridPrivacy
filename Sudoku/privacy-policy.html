Privacy Policy

Sudoku · Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains how Ideal Grid Studio (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the Sudoku mobile game (“the App”). Please replace the bracketed developer name and contact email below with your actual details before publishing this policy.

Information the App stores

The App stores game and preference data locally on your device. This can include your current level and difficulty, completed levels, stars and best times, tutorial status, settings such as theme, sound and haptics, and in-game wallet information such as coins, themes and booster-use counts. This information is used to save your progress and remember your preferences. It is not sent to us.

The App does not require you to create an account, and we do not use the App to collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, or payment-card details. The current version does not include advertising, analytics, or a third-party advertising SDK. Rewarded-video offers and purchase screens in the game are not connected to active ad delivery or payment processing.

Apple Game Center

On Apple devices, the App connects to Apple Game Center for its leaderboard feature. If you sign in and achieve a new best time, the App submits that time to Apple for the corresponding leaderboard. The App can also open the Game Center leaderboard screen. Apple handles Game Center sign-in, account information, leaderboard participation, and related service data under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Game Center password or operate a server that stores leaderboard data. Game Center is not used by the Android version.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell or share locally stored game progress with advertisers or other third parties. The App sends leaderboard scores and the requests needed for Game Center to Apple; it does not send locally stored progress to us or to advertisers. Your device's operating system and platform services may separately process information according to their own privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

Game and preference data remains in the App's local storage until you remove it using your device's app-data controls or uninstall the App. Device or platform backups may retain a copy according to your backup settings. Reset Progress in the App resets gameplay progress and coins; it does not erase every saved preference or wallet setting.

To request help with information handled through Apple Game Center, contact Apple or use the account and privacy controls Apple provides. We cannot delete information held by Apple on your behalf.

Security

The App keeps its game and preference data in the device's local app storage. We do not operate a server for this data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure; Apple Game Center's handling of its service data is governed by Apple's policies.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us using the details below so we can review the concern.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the App's features or data practices change. We will post the updated policy at this URL and revise the “Last updated” date above. Please review it periodically.
